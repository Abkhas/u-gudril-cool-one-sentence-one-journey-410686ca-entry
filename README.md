# One Sentence, One Journey

A complete story about a journey, told in exactly one sentence, for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty `u-gudril-cool-one-sentence-one-journey-410686ca`:

> The ferry left the harbour at first light with her wedding ring still in her pocket and her husband's voice still in her ears, and when the island rose out of the mist she threw the ring into the grey water and laughed for the first time in eleven years.

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (254 characters, one sentence, no title, no second sentence).
